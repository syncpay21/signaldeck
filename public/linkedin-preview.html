Motifs the deck will use

From BrandWorld.motifs — the recurring visual vocabulary.
Profile card grid — photo, name, headline, connection degree
Connection graph — node-and-edge people network rendered as a clean org chart, not a particle cloud
Endorsement badge strips — skill tags and recommendation counts
Career timeline — horizontal role progression rail
Job posting tile — company logo, role title, location, applicant count

Will not use — the anti-cliché list

From BrandWorld.avoid. SignalDeck won't render these even if Sonnet later suggests them.
  • Dark navy or black backgrounds with glowing neon accents
  • Purple-blue AI gradient aesthetics
  • Glassmorphism or frosted panel overlays
  • Generic "future of work" abstract visuals
  • Orb or particle neural-network motifs
  • Heavy editorial serif typography — LinkedIn is a grotesk-led utility product

Why this works

LinkedIn's entire visual equity lives in one blue (#0A66C2) on a white field — that's the brand, full stop. The card-and-feed layout mirrors the actual product surface professionals spend hours in daily, making the deck feel instantly native and credible to the audience.
