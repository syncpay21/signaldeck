Brand World — Luma

Delightful events start here.

A consumer events world built around the texture of real gatherings: invitation cards, calendar moments, venue photography, and the anticipation of showing up somewhere great. Warm creamy whites and rich violet-plum as the signature, evoking twilight golden hour and the glow of a well-lit room full of people.

Motifs the system will use

— Invitation card surfaces with soft shadow and slight tilt
— Calendar date stamps as hero elements
— Venue photography crops as full-bleed card covers
— RSVP confirmation ribbon or badge
— Guest list avatar clusters

Will not use — anti-cliché

  • Dark navy + purple gradient tech aesthetic
  • Glowing orbs or particle network backgrounds
  • Glassmorphism panels as primary UI
  • Generic calendar icon as hero motif
  • Corporate bento grid layouts
  • Cold blue enterprise SaaS palette

Why this works

Luma's job is to make people feel excited about showing up — the design mirrors that by borrowing from editorial event culture: warm paper tones, rich violet as a celebration accent, and invitation-card typography. Every surface should feel like something you want to hold and share, not a dashboard you have to manage.
